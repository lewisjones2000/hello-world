The silence

When someone flees conflict, they lose almost everything in hours. But one of the most invisible losses is communication. No credit. No signal. No way to say "I made it."

Phone Credit for Refugees puts credit directly into the hands of displaced people — through vetted NGO partners, with zero admin cut.
