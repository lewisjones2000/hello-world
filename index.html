Our Mission

Staying Connected is a Human Right

When people are forced to flee their homes, they lose almost everything — including the ability to stay in contact with loved ones, access emergency help, or navigate an unfamiliar country.

Phone Credit for Refugees is a registered non-profit that sources and distributes mobile phone credit directly to refugees in camps, transit zones, and resettlement centres around the world.

  • ✅ Contact with family separated by conflict
  • ✅ Access to emergency and legal services
  • ✅ Navigation tools and translation apps
  • ✅ Registration with aid organisations
"The first thing I did when I got phone credit was call my mother. She had not heard from me in four months. I cannot explain what that meant." — Amara, 27, arrived from Sudan
