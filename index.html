Our Mission

A phone isn't a luxury.
It's a lifeline.

When people flee conflict, disaster, or persecution, they lose almost everything overnight. But one of the cruelest losses is silence — being completely unreachable to the people who love them.

Phone Credit for Refugees sources mobile credit in bulk and delivers it directly to displaced people through trusted NGO partners on the ground. No admin fees. No middlemen. Just connection.
